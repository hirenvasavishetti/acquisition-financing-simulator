A further 180 purchase-commitment stress assumes incremental cash beyond ordinary operating costs and may double count. Neither stress bounds unreported exceptional FL commitments.

Actual covenant compliance additionally requires applicable lender definitions and certificates; confirmed availability requires updated borrowing bases, reserves and lender confirmation. Those last claims are outside this no-facility analytical route, not secretly satisfied.
