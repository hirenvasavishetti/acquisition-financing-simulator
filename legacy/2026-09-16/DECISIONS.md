implementation extends it; the full specification was never reduced to the gate.

Material decisions: standard-library HTTP dashboard avoids a large deployment
dependency; public XlsxWriter fallback preserves portable installation while the
bundled Artifact Tool creates the delivered workbook. Explicit annual-period
interest and simplified taxes favour inspectability. Capacity fixes the fee budget
and substitutes analytical equity; it cannot manufacture surplus borrowed cash.
Historical provenance consists of attributed factual extracts, not original source
bytes or human sign-off. Prior-year metrics remain visible without unnecessarily
blocking an explicit-growth forecast. No current cash/debt/contract gate was waived.

Precise outstanding historical work is listed in data/HISTORICAL_REVIEW.md and every
Evidence row. Model mapping also refuses unsupported target cash release and
historical quarterly seeds. SEC automated refresh needs a truthful contact string;
restricted OAS redistribution permission was not established. None of these gaps
prevents the fictional engineering pipeline.
