**Explain capacity.** Capacity is the maximum tested acquisition borrowing that passes every period's cash, leverage, coverage and maturity screens. Substitute explicitly labelled analytical equity for debt, recalculate fees according to the chosen convention, run the same engine, scan a grid and refine sampled transitions. I do not assume all feasible regions are connected. It measures model feasibility, not lender appetite.

**Explain operating sensitivity.** Reported cost classifications do not establish cost behaviour. Reconcile DKS EBITDA 1,874.341 and FL EBITDA 305 first.
