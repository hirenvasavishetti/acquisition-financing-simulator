Source verification and modelling conventions

Information cutoff: end of 15 May 2025. Primary-file extracts are machine-reviewed; hashes establish integrity, not factual accuracy. No independent human signoff is claimed. Costs classified in accounting statements do not establish economic cost behaviour. Public closing working capital is projected; Foot Locker receivables are held constant because their historical cash-flow movement is not separately disclosed.

Interest accrues on beginning-period principal. Payments, refinancing and maturity settlement occur at period end. A maturity inside a period is settled at that period end; this can defer a real cash need. Quarterly modelling reduces but does not eliminate that approximation.
