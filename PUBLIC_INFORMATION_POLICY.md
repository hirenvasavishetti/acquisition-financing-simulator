# Scope and evidence-policy amendment — 15 September 2026

This amendment is written before implementing the public-information case. The information cutoff remains the end of 15 May 2025. The existing strict assessment and its original requirements are preserved.

The additional case is an educational, conditional financing analysis, not a transaction-execution opinion. Outputs have separate dependencies and statuses:

1. **Verified historical calculations** require reviewed primary-source values and deterministic derivations. Missing or conflicting dependencies suppress the affected calculation.
2. **Conditional projections** require verified starting balances and relevant contractual provisions, plus explicit future assumptions. Future closing cash, rates, operating performance, elections and payoff amounts are projections, not facts to be verified retrospectively. A public disclosure may support an envelope for a private detail; the envelope and its limits must be recorded and tested.
3. **Unsupported outputs** remain blocked. Analytical leverage and coverage screens are not contractual covenants. The model will not assert legal compliance, lender appetite, executable financing, confirmed borrowing availability or an optimal actual transaction structure.

The selected public route retains DICK'S fixed notes, models contractual redemption of Foot Locker notes, and terminates Foot Locker's ABL. No existing facility contributes post-close borrowing availability. This removes borrowing-base certificates and retained-facility compliance calculations from the analytical capacity calculation, without claiming those requirements have been satisfied. Finance-lease cash flows may use disclosed aggregate maturities with an explicit interest-allocation approximation; instrument-level lease/legal conclusions stay blocked.

The opening bridge will forecast from the latest verified public cash and cash-flow information, with seasonal calibration, explicit prospective distributions/capex and a haircut for usable cash. No haircut is described as proof of legal cash availability. Funding comparisons will show uncertainty ranges and trade-offs. An unbounded material liability or missing essential contractual provision will prevent an overall company-specific preference even when some schedules calculate.

The strict recommendation status remains independent. A supported public-case comparison does not clear the strict gate. Any supported interpretation must begin: **“Under the stated public-information assumptions and analytical thresholds, the model indicates …”** Reproducibility and software tests demonstrate implementation behavior, not historical completeness.
